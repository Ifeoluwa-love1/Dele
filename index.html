About Us

Built on the Motor Road,
trusted across Lagos.

Detrotech Power Sinewave LTD is based on Agege Motor Road, Ladipo Shogunle, Oshodi/Isolo. We manufacture inverters, repair transformed-based inverters, and install solar systems.

Every unit that leaves our workshop is tested for a clean, pure sine wave output!

  • Manufacturing Custom & Standard inverters
  • Repairs Transformed-based inverter units
  • Solar Panel & system installation
  • Location Oshodi/Isolo, Lagos
What We Do

Three services, one power system.

From building the unit to keeping it running, we handle every stage of your power set up.

01 - Manufacturing

Inverter Manufacturing

Inverters built in-house and tuned for a stable, pure sine wave - sized to your home or business load , not a generic off-the-shelf spec.

02 - Repair

Transformed-Based Inverter Repair

Diagnosis and repair of transformer-based inverters. We fix the fault, not just the symptom.

03 - Solar

Solar Installation

Solar panel systems designed and installed to work alongside your inverter setup, cutting your dependence on fuel and grid downtime
